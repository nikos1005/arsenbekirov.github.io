31 tracks recorded with Grammy-winning producer Ian Brennan in Istanbul

In April 2026, Arsen Bekirov recorded 31 songs in Istanbul with Grammy-winning producer Ian Brennan (Tinariwen, Zomba Prison Project). The material spans the life cycle of Crimean Tatar song tradition — from lullabies to wedding songs — and includes a cycle in memory of the 1944 deportation.

2025
Qirim EP released on all platforms

Arsen's debut solo EP "Qirim" — nine original tracks bridging Crimean Tatar melodic tradition with contemporary ethno-pop. The music video for "Seni Seven" received the QARADENIZ Production award. The release is available on YouTube Music, Spotify, VK Music and other platforms.

2026
Arsen Bekirov Vocal School enrollment open in Simferopol

Arsen Bekirov, Honored Artist of the Republic of Crimea and the Republic of Tatarstan, lecturer at the Fevzi Yakubov Crimean Engineering and Pedagogical University, is now accepting students at his private vocal school. Individual lessons in pop, classical and folk vocals for children aged 6 and above and adults. Details on the Vocal School page.
