Arsen Bekirov is a Crimean Tatar tenor, Honored Artist of Crimea and Tatarstan, soloist of the Crimean State Philharmonic (since 2002) and of the Qirim ensemble. He performs in six languages. Author of the Qirim EP (2025), QARADENIZ award winner, he recorded 31 songs with Grammy-winning producer Ian Brennan in 2026. Founder of a vocal school in Simferopol.

Key Facts

2002

Soloist of the Crimean Philharmonic

Beginning of professional career at the Crimean State Philharmonic.

2005

Music College

Graduated from music college in vocal performance.

2006

First solo album "Eminim"

Debut program in four languages in Simferopol.

2008

Honored Artist of Crimea

Honorary title for contribution to Crimean Tatar culture.

2010

Donetsk Conservatory

Graduated from the Donetsk State Academy of Music.

2015

Tatarstan Tour

First concerts in Kazan and Naberezhnye Chelny.

2016

Honored Artist of Tatarstan

Federal recognition.

2025

Qirim EP & QARADENIZ Award

Debut EP, "Seni Seven" video wins award.

2026

31 songs with Ian Brennan (Istanbul)

Recording with Grammy-winning producer. Postgraduate degree completed.
