Biography

About the Artist

Arsen Bekirov is a Crimean Tatar tenor, Honored Artist of the Republic of Crimea and the Republic of Tatarstan. Soloist of the Crimean State Philharmonic (since 2002) and the legendary Crimean Tatar Qirim ensemble.

He first stepped on stage at the age of five and performs in six languages. In 2025, he released his debut EP "Qirim EP" and won the QARADENIZ Production award. In 2026, he recorded 31 songs with Grammy-winning producer Ian Brennan in Istanbul. Founder of the Arsen Bekirov Vocal School in Simferopol.
